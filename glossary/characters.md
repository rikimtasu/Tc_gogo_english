# Twinkle☆Crusaders GoGo! (PSP): Character Name Glossary

- **Source:** VNDB [v666 / chars](https://vndb.org/v666/chars#chars), pulled through the VNDB Kana API on 2026-11-21 (28 entries).
- **Checked against:** the game's own text pool (`GAMEINIT.CPK › scenario/textdata.bin`, 85,513 strings). "Hits" counts how many times the Japanese string appears anywhere in the script. "Label" means the string also exists on its own, which usually makes it a speaker-name label.
- **Status:** **LOCKED (Core Cast & Terms).** Decisions finalized on 2026-11-21 (provisional items in Appendix B remain subject to in-game UI verification).

**Conventions (Locked):**
- **Name order:** Given-name-first order in English prose and dialogue (e.g., *Synn Sakura*, *Nanaka Yuugiri*).
- **Honorifics:** Retain original Japanese honorifics (`-san`, `-senpai`, `-sensei`, `-chan`, `-kun`, `-sama`, and Akane's dialect `Kaichou-han`).
- **Romanization:** Modified Hepburn with doubled vowels (`Yuugiri`, `Kujou`) rather than macrons, matching VNDB.
- **Spelling Priority:** Official in-game/merchandise spellings and English VN community consensus take priority over phonetic variants (`ＳＡＫＵＲＡＳＹＮＮ` → *Synn*, `BE_AS_PAQY` / goods → *Paqy*, community/German root → *Rosenkreuz*, French pastry / goods → *Macaron*).

## Locked Naming & Style Decisions (2026-11-21)

1. **Honorifics & order:** Keep original Japanese honorifics (`-san`, `-senpai`, `-sensei`, `-chan`, `-kun`, `-sama`, `Kaichou-han`); use given-name-first order.
2. **ロロット・ローゼンクロイツ:** **Lolotte Rosenkreuz** (EBOOT confirms `Lolotte`; online/Wikipedia/Fandom/Twinkle Star Knights consensus prefers `Rosenkreuz`).
3. **マカロン:** **Macaron** (merchandise & community consensus prefer `Macaron`, overriding internal `MACAROON` asset IDs / VNDB).
4. **パッキー:** **Paqy** (confirmed by official goods and EBOOT `BE_AS_PAQY`).
5. **シン:** **Synn Sakura** (confirmed by in-script `ＳＡＫＵＲＡＳＹＮＮ` and VNDB/Fandom/TV Tropes).
6. **魔王 / 魔王様:** **Demon Lord** as a general noun/title; **Maou-sama** when addressed directly with the honorific.
7. **リ・クリエ:** **Re-Crea** (online lore consensus).
